rc-screening

rc-screening is a personal tool used by its developer only. It is not offered to the public.

It reads real-estate listing notification emails in the developer's own Gmail account, screens the listed properties against the developer's own investment criteria, and sends a summary email to the developer's own address. It does not send email to anyone else.

Privacy Policy

概要(日本語)

rc-screening は開発者本人だけが使う個人用のツールです。一般には提供していません。

開発者本人の Gmail に届く不動産の物件通知メールを読み、本人の投資基準で物件を絞り込み、結果を本人宛てのメールで送ります。本人以外にメールを送ることはありません。
